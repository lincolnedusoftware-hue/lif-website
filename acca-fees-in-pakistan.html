ACCA Fees in Pakistan 2026: Full Cost Breakdown

By Ammar Ali · Updated 7 September 2026

The total cost of ACCA in Pakistan has two parts: the fees you pay to ACCA in the UK (in pounds) and the tuition you pay to your institute (in rupees). For 2026, ACCA charges a one-time registration fee of £89 and a £140 annual subscription, with exam fees on top (ACCA Global). This guide breaks down every official fee in both GBP and rupees, gives you an honest estimate of the all-in cost from start to qualified, explains what tuition adds, and shows you the practical ways to keep the total down.

A quick note on rupee figures: ACCA fees are set in pounds, so the rupee cost depends on the exchange rate on the day you pay. The PKR figures below are illustrative at roughly PKR 375 to the pound (interbank, 2026). Always check the current rate before you budget.

How much does ACCA cost in Pakistan in 2026?

There is no single sticker price, because your cost depends on your starting point, how many exams you take, and your tuition provider. As a realistic guide, the fees you pay directly to ACCA for the full ACCA Qualification stage come to roughly £2,300 to £2,900 (about PKR 8.5 to 11 lakh at current rates), spread across three to four years. Tuition is separate and paid locally. For the complete picture of the qualification, see our full guide to ACCA in Pakistan.

What are the official ACCA fees for 2026?

These are the standard fees you pay directly to ACCA in the UK. They are the same wherever you study; only your tuition changes. Rupee amounts are illustrative at about PKR 375 per pound.

What does each fee actually mean?

  • Registration is a one-time fee to join ACCA as a student. You pay it once, at the start.
  • Annual subscription keeps your student status active. It is due every year on or before 1 January until you qualify.
  • Exam entry fees are paid per paper, each time you sit an exam. Enter early to pay the standard fee; leaving it late costs far more.
  • Exemption fees convert prior study into skipped papers. You pay the fee, but you save the time and stress of sitting that exam.

How much are the foundation-level (FIA) fees?

If you start after Matric or O-Levels, you begin with Foundations in Accountancy (FIA), and the foundation-level exam fees are lower than the ACCA Qualification fees above. Because these figures change, confirm the exact FIA per-paper fees on the ACCA website before you register. Our guide on starting ACCA after Matric explains the route.

What about tuition fees in Pakistan?

Tuition is what you pay your institute for teaching, materials and exam support, and it is separate from the ACCA fees above. It varies by provider and by whether you study in person or hybrid. Because it depends on your route and the current intake, we do not publish a fixed figure here. Book a free counselling session and we will give you an exact, up-to-date tuition cost, with no guesswork.

How do GBP fees convert to rupees?

Every fee above is set by ACCA in pounds, so your rupee cost rises or falls with the exchange rate. At roughly PKR 375 to the pound, a £160 exam works out around PKR 60,000, and the £140 annual subscription around PKR 52,500. If the rupee weakens, those numbers rise, so it is wise to pay fees promptly. Always confirm the live rate before you budget.

How can you reduce the total cost of ACCA?

  1. Enter exams early. Standard entry for an Applied Skills exam is £160, but late entry jumps to £409 (ACCA Global). Planning early can save you more than £240 per paper.
  2. Pass first time. Every resit means paying the exam fee again. Good preparation is the cheapest thing you can do.
  3. Claim every exemption you are entitled to. Exemptions cost a fee but remove a full exam, saving both money and months.
  4. Pay your annual subscription on time. Letting it lapse can mean re-registration and extra cost.
  5. Earn while you study. You can complete your practical experience alongside your exams, which helps you fund the fees as you go.

Is ACCA worth the cost?

For most students, yes. ACCA is recognised in 180 countries by more than 240,000 members (ACCA Global), and it opens roles across audit, tax, reporting and finance with employers including the Big 4 and major Pakistani companies. Weighed against a multi-year investment you can spread out and part-fund by working, the return is strong. Still deciding? Compare the paths in our guide on ACCA vs CA in Pakistan.

Frequently asked questions

How much does ACCA cost in Pakistan in 2026?

The fees paid directly to ACCA for the full ACCA Qualification stage come to roughly £2,300 to £2,900 (about PKR 8.5 to 11 lakh at current rates), spread over three to four years, plus separate local tuition.

What is the ACCA registration fee?

ACCA charges a one-time initial registration fee of £89, plus an annual subscription of £140 for 2026 to keep your student status active (ACCA Global).

How much are ACCA exam fees?

For the ACCA Qualification, standard entry is £160 for an Applied Skills exam, £282 for Strategic Business Leader, and £208 each for Strategic Business Reporting and the optional papers (ACCA Global). Foundation-level (FIA) exams cost less.

Are ACCA fees paid in pounds or rupees?

ACCA fees are set and paid in pounds. The rupee amount depends on the exchange rate on the day you pay, so always check the current rate before budgeting.

Does tuition cost extra?

Yes. Tuition is paid to your institute and is separate from ACCA’s fees. It varies by provider and route, so contact us for a current LIF figure.

How can I make ACCA cheaper?

Enter exams early to avoid the higher late fee, aim to pass first time, claim all exemptions you qualify for, pay your subscription on time, and earn while you study.

Scholarships, payment plans and keeping ACCA affordable

You can manage the cost of ACCA in Pakistan with a few practical levers. Register and pay ACCA’s fees on time to avoid late-entry penalties, plan your exam order so the cost spreads across sittings instead of landing all at once, and ask your institute about instalment or payment plans for tuition. Once you begin relevant work, some employers also part-fund later exams.

There is no single national ACCA scholarship in Pakistan, so treat any broad “scholarship” claim with caution and confirm it directly with the source.
