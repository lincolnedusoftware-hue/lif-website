What you study

Four core units, then your pathway

Everyone takes the same core units. Your optional units shape the degree you move on to.

Core

Developing English Language Skills (DELS 2.0)

Reading, listening, writing and speaking for university-level English.

Core

English for Academic Purposes (EAP 2.0)

Academic reading, writing, listening and speaking for degree study.

Core

Mathematics for University Study

The maths you need for business, computing and social science degrees.

Core

Research and Study Skills for University Study

Research, academic writing, referencing, critical thinking and presenting.

How you learn

Skill based and university-style

Small classes, real discussion and regular feedback. Each unit is assessed through exams, written assignments and presentations, with a pass mark of 40%. An overall 60% in EAP 2.0 meets the English requirement at many universities.
