About LIF

About Lincoln International Foundation

Building confident, future-ready leaders through a skills-based, university-style foundation year.

Our story

Built to give students a clearer route to university

Too many bright students lose time after school, unsure what comes next or which route will be accepted where. We started LIF to change that.

LIF gives students a faster, well-supported route to undergraduate study in Pakistan and abroad. Lincoln International Foundation is an NCC Education-accredited partner for its Level 3 International Foundation Diploma.

Mission and vision

What we stand for

Our mission
To prepare students for undergraduate study through a skills-based, university-style foundation year, with honest guidance at every step.
Our vision
To be the foremost institute that produces future leaders with a foundation in confidence, problem solving and innovation.
Our culture

What it feels like to study here

A small community where people look out for each other. This is what we hold ourselves to.

We know your name

Focused cohorts mean your teachers notice when you're stuck, and when you've done something brilliant.

We tell you straight

Honest advice about universities, grades and costs, even when it isn't what you hoped to hear.

We learn by doing

Presentations, debates and projects every week, so confidence becomes a habit.

We celebrate together

Independence Day, Eid, results day and the small wins in between.
