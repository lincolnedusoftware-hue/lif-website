Privacy policy

How Lincoln International Foundation handles the details you share through this website.

Apply NowBook a counselling session

Draft for review by LIF.

What we collect

When you book a counselling session we ask for your name, email address, contact number, school or college, current qualification, latest grades and a preferred date. When you apply we ask for your name, email address, contact number, current qualification and preferred pathway. We use these only to arrange your session, process your application and advise you about admission.

How it reaches us

The form opens WhatsApp with your details filled in. Nothing is sent until you press send in WhatsApp, and WhatsApp's own privacy policy applies to that message.

Cookies and tracking

This website does not use cookies, analytics or advertising trackers.

Your choices

To see, correct or delete the details you have shared, email admissions@lincolninternationalfoundation.com.
