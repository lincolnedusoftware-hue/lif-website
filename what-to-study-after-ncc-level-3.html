What Can You Study After NCC Level 3? (Business, Accounting, Law and More)

After NCC Level 3 you move straight into Year 1 of an undergraduate degree, in fields ranging from business and accounting to law, computing, engineering and the sciences, at more than 120 universities and institutions across 20+ countries (NCC Education). The foundation year is a bridge, and where it takes you depends on the electives you choose and the degree you are aiming at. Here is what you can study next, and how to pick.

What can you study after NCC Level 3?

NCC Level 3 is a general foundation for higher education, so it opens a wide range of degrees rather than locking you into one. Graduates typically progress into degrees in business and management, accounting and finance, law, computing and IT, engineering, and the health sciences. The exact options depend on the university and the specific degree’s entry requirements, but the diploma is built to prepare you for first-year undergraduate study across these areas.

If you are still getting to grips with the qualification itself, our guide on what NCC Level 3 is explains how the foundation year works.

Which degrees does the foundation year lead to?

Your electives point you toward a field. At LIF the electives include Law, Introductory Accounting and International Business, which line up naturally with the most popular degree routes for Pakistani students.

Can you go straight into university Year 1 after NCC Level 3?

Yes. That is the whole point of the qualification. NCC Level 3 is designed as direct entry to the first year of an undergraduate degree, so you do not lose a year. Because it is Ofqual-regulated and recognised by universities as an alternative to A-Levels (NCC Education), you apply for Year 1 the same way an A-Level student would.

Is accounting a strong route after NCC Level 3?

It is one of the most popular, and for good reason. If you take the Introductory Accounting elective and enjoy it, you can progress into an accounting and finance degree, or into a professional route like ACCA.

How do you choose the right subject after the foundation year?

Work backwards from the career you want, not just the subject you liked at school. Ask three questions: what degree does that career need, which universities offer it and accept NCC Level 3, and which electives strengthen that application. This is exactly what a counselling session is for; an adviser helps you line up electives, degree and university so the foundation year points precisely where you want to go.

Frequently asked questions

What can you study after NCC Level 3?

You can progress into undergraduate degrees in business, accounting and finance, law, computing and IT, engineering and the sciences. NCC Level 3 is a general foundation that leads into Year 1 of a range of degrees at 120+ universities and institutions in 20+ countries.

Does NCC Level 3 let you go straight into university?

Yes. It is designed as direct entry to the first year of an undergraduate degree, and universities recognise it as an alternative to A-Levels, so you do not lose a year.

Can I study accounting or do ACCA after NCC Level 3?

Yes. The Introductory Accounting elective is a strong base for an accounting and finance degree or a professional route such as ACCA.

Can I study law after the foundation year?

Yes. With the Law elective, NCC Level 3 can lead into an LLB or related law degree, subject to the university's own entry requirements.

How do I choose what to study after NCC Level 3?

Work backwards from your target career: identify the degree it needs, the universities that offer it and accept NCC Level 3, and the electives that strengthen your application. A counselling session helps you align all three.
